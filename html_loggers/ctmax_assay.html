ZEAL CTmax Logger

Changes are kept in this browser

1Session details

Applies to every assay in this session and is written to each row of the export.

2Groups and tube layout

Enter a population or species, a treatment, or both. Rows left blank are dropped. Tube assignments are randomized across all 12 positions, including the thermometer.

3Run the assay

Before the ramp starts, the grid shows which group goes in each tube so you can load the rack. Once you press Ramp started, group identities are hidden and tubes are scored by number only. Record the thermometer reading as each individual reaches CTmax; the time is stamped automatically and can be corrected.

4Export

One row per individual. Population and treatment columns are included only if at least one group uses them.
